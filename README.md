## About This Repository

This repository is a curated portfolio of my work across product prototyping and quantitative analytics. 

My background is not one straight line. It started in complex institutional credit risk workflows, grew through data pipelines and predictive modeling, and now extends into independent, full stack product prototyping. The goal of this portfolio is to demonstrate how I tackle messy, high friction domains and translate them into structured, clinical, and actionable digital systems.

The portfolio is split into two halves:
1. **Live Product Prototypes:** Concept operating systems built to translate complex logic into premium, mobile-first workflows.
2. **Quantitative Analytics:** Structured architectures detailing end-to-end machine learning and risk models.

## 🚀 Live Product Prototypes

These are exploratory web interfaces and concept prototypes built using Next.js 16, Tailwind v4, and AI integrations to visualize how data, context, and operational workflows can be merged into cohesive systems.

### [Yukti OS](https://yukti-os.vercel.app)
**Context-First Geriatric Care Concept**
An exploratory interface and workflow concept for geriatric care. Built to visualize how clinical medical data and high frequency patient context (daily habits) could be integrated into a single system using an empathetic AI persona.

### [Quant OS](https://quant-os.vercel.app/)
**Quantitative Knowledge Graph**
A spatial learning environment and 2D physics map for my analytics and quantitative learning. Built to make dense risk models, Basel regulations, and data science concepts easier to navigate and connect.

### [Curiosity OS](https://curiosity-os.vercel.app)
**Digital Laboratory & Facilitation Layer**
A conceptual learning layer and workflow interface for educators. Built on a 147 node atomic knowledge graph to translate pedagogical theory into practical classroom facilitation tools.

### [Mila](https://meetmila.vercel.app)
**Psychology-Backed Social Ecosystem**
A curated dating and community prototype. Built to replace infinite swiping with a strict 3 layer matching algorithm, utilizing a custom 80 profile MECE psychological matrix.

### [Pause](https://pause-lac.vercel.app)
**FMCG & D2C Strategic Framework**
A cinematic strategic exploration and high fidelity visionary framework of modern product growth, demonstrating mastery of identifying FMCG whitespaces and economic waterfall logic.
